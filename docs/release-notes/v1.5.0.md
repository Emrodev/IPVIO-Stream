## Highlights
- Fixed: many missing series now appear, each in its own category
- Fixed: Xtream playlists load all their movies and series
- New: "Sound levelling" makes quiet live channels as loud as streaming apps
- Playlist switch keeps the screen, and the TV guide finds more channels

## New
- **Sound levelling:** Settings > Audio & subtitles > "Sound levelling", off by default. Live channels are often much quieter than streaming apps; "Default" raises them without clipping, evens out loud and quiet parts and lifts dialogue a little. Other modes: "Clear speech", "Night mode" and "Custom". Sound passed through unchanged to a receiver (Dolby, DTS) is not processed.
- **Channel keys:** the remote's P+ and P- keys switch to the next and the previous channel in the player.
- **Diagnostics:** after loading a playlist, Settings > Diagnostics > Log shows how many entries the provider's list has, and how many were left out and why (divider lines, language filter, content mode, parental control, ignored categories, broken titles). This helps when the app shows fewer titles than your provider.

## Improved
- **Playlist switch:** when your device has enough memory, the current screen stays and the list changes in place, without the loading screen.
- **TV guide:** channels without a guide ID, or with an ID that differs only slightly from the guide's, now get their programme by a similar ID or by the channel name. A name used by several channels in the guide is only matched when the language fits, so you get no programme rather than a wrong one.
- Large playlists are read faster.
- The playlist "Edit" window never gets taller than the screen; "Apply" and "Cancel" stay visible.
- An unexpected loading error names the place in the app, so a screenshot is enough for a bug report.

## Fixed
- **Missing series (Xtream and Stalker playlists):** series with the same name were shown as one: for example the same series in a German and an English category, "The Office (US)" and "(UK)", or two series from different years. Each one is now its own series and appears in its own category. A series you watched before keeps its progress and its place in My list.
- **Missing movies and series on Xtream:** some providers' M3U links deliver only part of the movies or series. The app now loads the whole list through the provider's API. Press "Reload" once on the playlist.
- Phone page: "Upload" for a playlist file, a list or a VPN configuration did nothing. It works again, and a rejected upload shows the reason.

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature). No manual install needed if you use the in-app update.
- You may see more series than before, and some series now appear in each of their categories.
- If you watched the second version of a same-name series before, its progress stays with the first one.
- Most of this version is not yet tested on a TV device; the speed gain was measured on a computer only. Test reports are welcome.
- Lists import: Trakt export files can no longer be imported (Letterboxd and MyAnimeList still can). Lists you imported from Trakt before stay.
- The VPN has no kill switch: if the connection drops, traffic continues without it.

## Highlights
- New: Trakt sync of history and watchlist (Settings > Import & Trakt)
- New: "Combine seasons" joins series a provider lists season by season
- Live TV player: fewer buttons, rewind on catch-up channels, your own button order
- Fixed: skipping and resuming in MKV movies without an index

## New
- **Trakt:** Settings > Import & Trakt > "Connect Trakt" shows an address and a QR code. Open it on your phone (same Wi-Fi), enter the 4-digit code shown on the TV, sign in to Trakt, then copy the text the Trakt page shows and paste it on the phone page. You need a free Trakt account.
- **Trakt sync:** right after connecting, at every app start (at most every 6 hours) and with "Sync now", the app reads your watched movies and episodes and your watchlist from Trakt. Watched titles in your playlist get their check mark, and your history and watchlist appear as the categories "Trakt · Watched" and "Trakt · Watchlist" in Movies and Series. The app only reads: nothing in your Trakt account is changed, and progress you already have in the app is never changed. "Disconnect Trakt" ends the sign-in.
- **Combine seasons:** Settings > Playlists > Series > "Combine seasons" (off by default). Some Xtream providers list every season as its own series ("Show S01", "Show S02"); switched on, they become one series with all its seasons.
- **Live TV player buttons:** Settings > Live TV > Player buttons: choose which buttons the Live TV player shows and in which order.
- **Tunneled playback (Live TV):** Settings > Player > Source & quality, off by default. Picture and sound run through one hardware path, which can help on boxes where Live TV stutters or picture and sound drift apart.
- **Genre categories in Live TV:** a switch in Settings > Live TV. Off shows only your provider's own categories.

## Improved
- Live TV player: fewer buttons on screen (Left still opens the channel list, Right the TV guide). On channels with an archive (catch-up) there is a rewind button.
- Phones and tablets: tap or drag on the timeline to jump to a position; tapping a Live TV category opens it.
- Search: with the filter "Persons", Down now reaches the people results.
- Movies and Series: the "Recently added" row shows the same titles as the "Recently added" page, and the Genre filter shows the genre you opened.
- Sports alerts: the delay can be set up to 120 seconds, so an alert does not spoil a goal on a stream that runs behind.
- M3U playlists: categories written in other ways (for example `tvg-group` or `#EXTGRP`) are now found instead of all landing in one category.
- Settings: "Import lists" is now "Import & Trakt".

## Fixed
- MKV movies without a seek index: skipping, number-key jumps and "Resume" no longer restart the movie from the beginning.
- Saved playlists could vanish on some boxes after a restart, when the secure key could not be read for a moment. The app no longer replaces the key, and if the saved list cannot be read the home screen says so.
- Switching "Movies & series" back on in a playlist's content mode now brings them back even while the list is loading.

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature). No manual install needed if you use the in-app update.
- Trakt sync reads only: what you watch in the app is not sent to Trakt yet.
- Titles are found in your playlist by name and year, so a title named differently by your provider may not be matched.
- Not device tested yet: the Trakt sign-in and sync, the new Live TV player buttons, tunneled playback and the MKV seeking are new. Test reports are welcome.
- Trakt export files are still not imported (see version 1.5.0); Letterboxd and MyAnimeList files still are.
- Arabic, Dutch and Polish still show the old label for the import section until they are translated.

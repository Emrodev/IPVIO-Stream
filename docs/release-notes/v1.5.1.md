## Highlights
- New: sign in to Trakt from your phone (Settings > Import & Trakt > "Connect Trakt")
- Trakt sign-in is not device tested yet; watch history is not synced yet
- Settings: "Import lists" is now "Import & Trakt"
- Your playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept on update

## New
- **Trakt sign-in:** Settings > Import & Trakt > "Connect Trakt" shows an address and a QR code for your phone, on the same Wi-Fi. Open it, sign in to Trakt, copy the text the Trakt helper page shows and paste it on the phone page. The TV then stores the sign-in on the device. If the device cannot store it securely, nothing is saved. You need a free Trakt account.

## Improved
- **Settings:** "Import lists" is now "Import & Trakt". The import page on the phone has the same name.

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature). No manual install needed if you use the in-app update.
- Not device tested yet: the Trakt sign-in is new. Test reports are welcome.
- The sign-in does not sync your watch history or watchlist yet.
- Trakt export files are still not imported (see version 1.5.0). Letterboxd and MyAnimeList files still are.
- Arabic, Dutch and Polish still show the old label for the import section until they are translated.

## Highlights
- Trailer on the detail page, centred while it plays
- Profile PIN with a lock icon
- Faster Sports Hub and search
- Fixed: Live TV and Movies and Series category navigation

## New
- **Trailers on the detail page:** the trailer plays behind the header after two seconds (Settings > General > "Play trailers automatically", on by default). While it plays, only Play and "Stop trailer" stay on screen; Stop brings everything back. With the setting off, "Trailer" asks whether to play in the app or open YouTube.
- **Trailer on the home screen:** optional (off by default), the trailer of the selected title plays behind the big picture.
- **Profile PIN:** "Add PIN" or "Change PIN" on each profile; a profile with a PIN shows a lock icon and asks for the PIN when it is opened.
- **Trakt sync interval:** choose on every app start, every 6 or 12 hours, daily, weekly, or only when you tap "Sync now".
- **Genre rows in Live TV** (Sport, Movies, Documentaries, Kids, News, Music) can be moved like your provider's categories.
- **Diagnostics:** "Open on phone" shows the whole report as text on your phone, where you can copy it.
- **Playlist lists** on Home, Movies and Series close with Left or Right as well as Back.

## Improved
- Settings: the page moves only when a control is cut off; Down at the last control goes to the Back arrow.
- Sports Hub: each league's games appear as soon as that league answers.
- Search: the first suggestion or recent search is reachable from the keyboard with Up; result covers no longer repeat the kind word.
- Movies and Series: the same film from different provider entries is shown once in a category grid and in "Top rated" (when the provider sends its TMDB id).
- Lists: the focused row stays in the middle of the screen while you move through it.

## Fixed
- Live TV: Up on the first category goes to the last one, and Down on the last goes to the first.
- Movies and Series: Left from the titles grid returns to the category you came from.
- Sports Hub: "Live results" was listed twice in its settings.
- Phone sign-in for Trakt: the login opens in its own tab, so the code page stays open.

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept. The playlist cache is rewritten once in a new format on the next load.
- Not measured: how long search and the Sports Hub take on the first open after the app starts.
- Playlist reload shows no percentage yet for Xtream providers, because their total size is not known in advance.
- Most of this release has not been tested on a device yet by the developers; the trailer also depends on the device and on YouTube.
- Some newer texts may still show in German in some languages (open).

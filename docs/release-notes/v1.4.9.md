## Highlights
- New: series downloads, download a whole season or pick single episodes
- New: choose playlists per section (Live TV, Movies, Series)
- New: search categories including People, and a new person page with filmography
- New: Sports Hub tidier, OK on a running game starts its channel

## New
- **Series downloads:** on every series, "Download season" queues all episodes of the shown season. "Pick episodes" opens the episode list with a check box per episode; the checked episodes are queued together. One short message confirms the queue. Finished downloads play from the device, also offline (sidebar, Downloads tab).
- **Playlists per section:** in Live TV, Movies and Series you can pick any number of playlists. Playlists that do not fit the section are greyed out with the reason. The choice is kept after a restart.
- **Search:** new layout with categories and suggestions. "People" finds actors and directors (needs your TMDB key); a tap opens their page with profile, biography and filmography, filter included.
- **Sports Hub:** tidier schedule and channel tab, icons in the tab row, and "Back" from a channel returns to the hub.
- **Live TV channel keys:** new setting "Up goes to the next channel" (off by default).
- **Diagnostics:** the log shows how movies and series were loaded and when each screen opened.

## Improved
- Playlist menus: every playlist can be unticked, and "All playlists" toggles between all and none.
- Settings > Playlists shows the type and content mode of each playlist.
- "Because you watched" on the home screen appears sooner.

## Fixed
- Movies and series missing on some Xtream playlists: the full list is loaded through the provider's API when the M3U link only has live channels. Press "Reload" once on the playlist.
- The playlist menus in Live TV, Movies and Series load a playlist next to the main list instead of replacing it.

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature).
- Most of this version is not yet tested on many devices. Test reports are welcome.
- Some providers' streams fail to download: their server certificate does not match the host name. The app does not skip the check.
- The VPN has no kill switch: if the connection drops, traffic continues without it.
